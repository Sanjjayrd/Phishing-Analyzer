    What this tool can and cannot do

    It cannot

    • Prove that an email is malicious. It matches the text against known patterns. A suspicious-looking message may be genuine, and a carefully crafted attack may show very few signs.
    • Prove that an email is safe. A low score only means that nothing on the checklist matched. It is not a clean bill of health.
    • Detect everything. It cannot see images, QR codes, real attachment contents, or anything hidden behind a link.

    Please always

    • Never click a link or open an attachment to see what happens. Testing a suspected phishing email is exactly what the attacker wants.
    • Verify important messages independently - use the organisation's official website or app, or a phone number you already have. Never a contact detail taken from the email itself.
    • Report it to your IT or security team, or to a national anti-phishing service, then delete it.

    This is an educational triage tool for building your own judgement - not a malware scanner, an email gateway, or a substitute for professional security advice.
